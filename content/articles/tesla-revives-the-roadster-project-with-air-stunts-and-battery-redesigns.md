## Tesla Seeks a New Spark for the Roadster

Nine years after its initial attempt to resurrect the Roadster, Tesla is making another push to bring the vehicle to light. CEO Elon Musk recently demonstrated a show car capable of performing mid-air tricks using decades-old stunt technology, returning to California's entertainment industry to present the vehicle.

Musk now claims that the Roadster will enter production by 2028. However, the executive has a established history of overpromising timelines. During a 2017 launch event, Musk claimed the vehicle was actively being made. That effort left customers empty-handed, including individuals who placed $50,000 deposits and participants in the company's referral program who were in line for free cars. Notably, Sam Altman faced difficulties last year attempting to recover his deposit money.
