## Legal Foundations Shift
On July 15 2026, South Korea’s Ministry of Economy and Finance announced the National Asset Basic Act, which for the first time in 76 years incorporates digital assets into the legal framework that governs public wealth. The act follows Japan’s parliamentary vote to reclassify cryptocurrencies as financial products on the same day. The move signals a shift from treating crypto as a payment method to recognizing it as a financial asset within the state’s asset base.
