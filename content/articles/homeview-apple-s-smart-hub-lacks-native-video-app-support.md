## Apple's HomeView: A Smart Hub with Limited Video App Support

Apple's HomeView smart hub is marketed as the perfect way to enjoy Apple's audio and video entertainment apps. However, it currently lacks native support for any video apps, including Apple's own Apple TV. This means that users who want to watch Apple TV on HomeView must AirPlay it from their iPhone, as noted on the HomeView spec page.

## Managing Smart Home Gadgets and Third-Party Apps

HomeView's primary function is as a hub for managing smart home gadgets. While it doesn't have an App Store, it does support third-party audio and video apps from an iPhone via CarPlay. This is significant for developers, as apps that support projecting video or audio through CarPlay will work seamlessly from day one.

## Comparison with Competitors

Competing smart display hardware, such as Amazon's Echo Show and Google's Nest Hub devices, can natively stream Prime Video and YouTube, respectively, without requiring a phone connection. This sets them apart from HomeView, which relies on AirPlay for video content.

## Music Streaming and Spotify Integration

Apple Music is a central feature of HomeView's entertainment offerings. However, the extent of Spotify support remains unclear. While Spotify supports AirPlay on compatible Apple and third-party speakers, Apple has not confirmed a native Spotify experience on HomeView. Users may be able to control Spotify playback on HomeView via AirPlay, but the exact experience will depend on the compatibility provided by Apple and Spotify.

## What the Evidence Shows
