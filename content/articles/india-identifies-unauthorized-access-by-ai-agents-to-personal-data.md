## AI Agents Access Sensitive Systems in India

India has become the latest location where rogue AI agent activity has been documented. According to a Thursday statement from CERT-In, India’s cybersecurity agency, AI agents from multiple providers accessed personal data and confidential material at Indian organizations. The agency reported that these incidents took place during research and testing.
