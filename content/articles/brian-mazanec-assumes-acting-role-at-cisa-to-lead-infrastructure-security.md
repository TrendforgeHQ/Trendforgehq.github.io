## Background of the Appointment
The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) named Brian Mazanec acting executive assistant director for infrastructure security.  He will oversee CISA’s Infrastructure Security Division, which works with government and private‑sector partners to identify vulnerabilities, manage risks, and improve resilience across the nation’s 16 critical infrastructure sectors.  Mazanec brings more than two decades of experience in risk assessment, critical infrastructure protection, and public‑private coordination, according to CISA.  Before joining the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), he led the strategic warfare, security, and intelligence portfolio at the Government Accountability Office and worked on weapons of mass destruction issues at the Defense Intelligence Agency, the Office of the Deputy Assistant Secretary of Defense for Nuclear Matters, and the Joint Staff.

## Why the Move Matters
Mazanec’s prior role at HHS involved directing the department’s response to major cybersecurity incidents affecting the healthcare sector, including the 2024 Change Healthcare ransomware attack that disrupted healthcare operations nationwide.  The appointment places a health‑sector cyber veteran in charge of securing all 16 critical infrastructure sectors, a shift that expands his focus from a single sector to the full spectrum of national infrastructure.  CISA has also introduced the CI Fortify initiative this year, encouraging critical infrastructure organizations to remain resilient to successful cyber attacks and to plan for outages that could sever connections to internet, telecommunications and other technology services.

## Immediate Focus and Partnerships
As acting executive assistant director, Mazanec will focus on risk management and resilience, partnering with members of the critical infrastructure community.  He has also worked with CISA and the FBI on joint cybersecurity advisories and engaged critical infrastructure partners.  The move follows CISA’s earlier appointment of Brian Bonacci to lead its emergency communications division and the succession of Butera for acting CISA director.

## What to Watch Next
The new leadership structure signals an intensified effort to blend health‑sector experience with broad infrastructure protection.  Observers will note how Mazanec leverages his public‑private coordination skills to strengthen collaboration across sectors and whether the CI Fortify initiative gains traction under his oversight.  The appointment also highlights CISA’s ongoing focus on securing defense critical infrastructure that supports military bases and operations through partnerships such as those with the Army.
