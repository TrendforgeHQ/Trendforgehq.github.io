## Apple's Siri AI: Catching Up and Looking Ahead

Apple's Siri AI has recently undergone a significant update, bringing it closer to the capabilities of OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports that CEO John Ternus is pushing AI chief Craig Federighi to accelerate the Siri roadmap and deliver a powerful AI agent that can compete with OpenAI's Dots and Meta's Muse. This pressure comes as Apple acknowledges that it needs to keep pace with rivals who have already deployed AI agents capable of autonomous, multi-step tasks.

## The Current State of Siri

The latest version of Siri, released just last month, represents a substantial improvement. It can now interact like other AI chatbots, answering more in-depth questions and demonstrating increased on-screen awareness and personal context. Siri can search through photos, emails, and texts, providing a more comprehensive understanding of user needs. However, despite these advancements, Siri still feels dated, especially when compared to Meta's Muse, which can autonomously complete personal tasks such as booking flights and shopping online.

## The Race for Agentic AI

Apple is expected to make Siri more agentic, enabling it to perform ongoing tasks rather than just one-offs. This shift is crucial to meet consumer and market demands. The company is exploring ways to give Siri fine-grained control over its first-party apps, allowing it to perform actions like rotating and cropping images in Photos and organizing Safari tabs. This functionality could extend to third-party apps, enabling Siri to place orders through Grubhub or book reservations through OpenTable.

## Challenges and Opportunities

Apple's internal urgency around Siri reflects a broader shift in its approach to AI competition. The company recognizes that if third-party apps optimize for OpenAI and Meta agents first, it risks becoming a second-class citizen in the AI ecosystem it aims to control. To maintain its position, Apple needs Siri to become genuinely useful, not just incrementally better. This challenge is compounded by the fact that Siri currently lags behind in AI capabilities, despite Apple's vast user base and trusted brand.

## What to Watch

As Apple races to enhance Siri's agentic capabilities, several key developments are worth monitoring. First, the company is expected to introduce significant improvements to Siri in the coming months, including enhanced control over first-party apps. Second, Apple's iOS 28 development is underway, with a focus on integrating Siri with third-party apps. Lastly, Apple's ability to balance rapid AI development with its commitment to trust and privacy will be crucial. The company must deliver on its AI promises while maintaining the security and privacy standards its users expect.
