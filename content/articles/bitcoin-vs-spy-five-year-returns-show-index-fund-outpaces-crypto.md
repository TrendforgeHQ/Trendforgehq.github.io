Because Bitcoin’s price movements are steep, a loss can take many years to recover. For retirees who need regular income, a cash payout from dividends can be critical. A significant drawdown in Bitcoin could leave a retiree unable to replace lost gains, a risk that planners term sequence‑of‑returns risk.
