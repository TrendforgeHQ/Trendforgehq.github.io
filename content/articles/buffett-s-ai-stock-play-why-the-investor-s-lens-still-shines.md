## Long‑Term Holding Versus Market Timing
Warren Buffett’s investment creed centers on selecting businesses with durable competitive advantages, predictable earnings, and managers who allocate capital wisely. This principle is reiterated by both Yahoo Finance and The Motley Fool, which echo Buffett’s preference for buying and holding stocks indefinitely. Buffett has publicly stated that his favorite holding period is “forever,” and he cautions investors against attempting to time the market. A related AOL article explains that no one can reliably predict whether a bear market will occur, its duration, or the speed of a subsequent recovery. These remarks reinforce the idea that trying to sell before a crash could cause investors to miss out on a rebound, and that prices may rise by the time a buyer reenters the market.
