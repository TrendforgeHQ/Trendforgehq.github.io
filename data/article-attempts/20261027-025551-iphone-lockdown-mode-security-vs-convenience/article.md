## What Lockdown Mode Does
Apple added Lockdown Mode in iOS 16 to give users an “extreme” security layer. It disables FaceTime calls, blocks connections to unsecured Wi‑Fi networks, turns off 2G and 3G support, and stops phone‑call notifications from an Apple Watch. The changes are intentional and designed to block known attack vectors, as the company’s Security Bounty program offers a 100 percent bonus for anyone who finds a way to bypass the feature.

## How to Turn It On and Off
Enabling Lockdown Mode is a one‑step process. Open Settings, navigate to Privacy & Security, scroll to Lockdown Mode, tap Turn On & Restart, and confirm with your passcode. The same path lets you disable it again. The feature is available on iPhones running iOS 16 or newer, on iPads with iPadOS 16, on Apple Watches with watchOS 10, and on Macs with macOS 13 Ventura.

## Who Needs It?
Apple identifies journalists, activists, and politicians as the primary users likely to face targeted spyware such as Pegasus from NSO Group. The company notes that the inconveniences caused by Lockdown Mode are not worth the extreme protection for most people, implying that everyday users may find the trade‑off excessive.

## The Trade‑off
Lockdown Mode provides maximum security by restricting many common functions, but it also reduces convenience. FaceTime is blocked, recent contacts can still call, and wireless connectivity is limited to secure networks. Users lose the ability to receive call notifications on an Apple Watch and to automatically join open Wi‑Fi hotspots. These changes are deliberate, as Apple wants to keep the feature simple and reversible.

## Bottom Line
For most iPhone users, the daily loss of features outweighs the benefit of extreme spyware protection. Those working in high‑risk environments—such as journalists or activists—may find Lockdown Mode a valuable tool, especially given Apple’s willingness to double its Security Bounty for bypass attempts. The feature is straightforward to enable and disable, but its restrictive nature means it is best suited for a niche audience rather than general consumers.
