## T-Mix Plans Bundle Wireless Service with Streaming Subscriptions

T-Mobile is launching new "T-Mix" plans that combine wireless service with subscriptions to popular streaming platforms such as Netflix, Apple TV, YouTube Premium, and Xbox Game Pass Ultimate. These bundles aim to provide integrated entertainment value alongside mobile connectivity. The Standard tier begins at $55 per month for one line and one "Pick," allowing users to select initial perks and later modify their benefits as needs evolve.

## Swappable Perks and Change Windows

The structure of these plans centers on swappable benefits. Customers can choose their initial perks at the start of their plan and then exchange them for alternative options as their requirements shift. However, the frequency of switching depends on the specific benefit selected. Some perks permit changes at any time, while others lock the selected "Picks" for a quarter or longer. Notably, certain services may charge if a customer attempts to switch outside the designated change window.

## Additional Context from Independent Testing

Complementing T-Mobile's rollout, EE has also introduced truly unlimited data plans on the UK's leading mobile network. These plans have been rated by independent testing experts for the sixth consecutive year. The 4G Smart Plan offers two swappable benefits, enabling users to customize their perks at the outset and swap them as their needs change. Business users receive additional advantages including Upgrade Anytime and BT Sport, while 5G plans include Mobile Iron Threat Defence to protect against security risks across devices, networks, and applications.

What the Evidence Shows: The evidence confirms T-Mobile's T-Mix plans bundle wireless service with streaming subscriptions and feature swappable perks with variable change windows, while EE's unlimited plans on the UK's best network offer similar flexibility through swappable benefits and business-focused perks.
