## Asset Holdings and Valuation

Hyperscale Data, Inc. (NYSE American: GPUS), an artificial intelligence data center company headquartered at 11411 Southern Highlands Parkway, Suite 190, Las Vegas, NV 89141, announced its balance sheet holdings. As of October 18, 2026, the company held 210.0129 Bitcoin. This position represented an aggregate value of approximately $17.2 million, based on a Bitcoin closing price of $82,018.

In addition to its digital asset reserves, Hyperscale Data held approximately $38.1 million in cash and restricted cash. Across separate disclosures, the company's holdings were reported at $56 million, equal to 153% of its common-stock market capitalization.

## Corporate Restructuring and Divestiture Timeline

Hyperscale Data has outlined strategic steps regarding its corporate structure. The company expects the divestiture of ACG to occur in 2027. ACG is actively engaged in private credit and structured finance operations through its licensed lending subsidiary, Ault Lending, LLC.

Upon the completion of the ACG divestiture, Hyperscale Data's operations will focus on owning and operating data centers designed to support high-performance computing services. The company will also remain a holder of digital assets and retain its third wholly owned subsidiary, Omnipresent Robotics, LLC.

Prior to these updates, on December 23, 2024, the company issued 1,000,000 shares of a newly designated Series F Exchangeable Preferred Stock. These shares were distributed to all common stockholders and holders of Series C Preferred Stock on an as-converted basis.

## What the Evidence Shows

Hyperscale Data maintains significant reserves in cash, restricted cash, and Bitcoin relative to its equity valuation. The company is actively pursuing a transition toward high-performance computing data centers, anchored by the planned 2027 divestiture of ACG and the retention of its robotics subsidiary and digital assets.
