## Expected Announcement Date and Cost-of-Living Projections

This annual announcement will provide workers and retirees with a clearer understanding of how their benefits and tax obligations are expected to shift in the coming year. The official changes rely heavily on key inflation data released by the Bureau of Labor Statistics, including September inflation metrics, to set the final parameters.

Among the details included in the October release, the annual cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) remains the most closely watched metric. The COLA directly determines the bump in monthly checks that beneficiaries will see starting with their January 2027 payments.
