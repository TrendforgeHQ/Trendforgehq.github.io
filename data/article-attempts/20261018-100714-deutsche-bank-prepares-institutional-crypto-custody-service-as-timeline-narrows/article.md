## Deutsche Bank Prepares Crypto Custody Launch

Deutsche Bank has shortened its timeline for launching a cryptocurrency custody service, stating in an October update that the release is now just weeks away. The service will focus initially on Germany. This update follows an announcement on October 16, 2026, outlining plans to expand the bank's crypto custody offerings into Asian financial centers in 2027.

Under this business model, custodians hold digital assets that institutional clients—such as corporate treasuries or pension funds—have already chosen to purchase. By managing and holding the cryptographic keys, the bank enables these institutions to hold cryptocurrency without needing to handle the private keys themselves. Deutsche Bank gains a new fee business, while German institutions receive easier access to digital assets. However, Bitcoin and Ether experience buying pressure only if those institutional clients place actual orders.

## Institutional Impact and Market Context

Because custody banks typically charge a small annual fee, the financial sustainability of this business depends primarily on clients entrusting them with significant volumes of assets. Client demand remains a key factor motivating institutions to enter the sector. Following the initial launch, Deutsche Bank plans to introduce additional services, including tokenized deposits that record traditional bank deposits on a blockchain, digital bonds, and stablecoin offerings, though specific dates for these offerings have not been scheduled.

More major banks are likely to adopt similar services as rules ease and client requests grow. The Basel Committee, which establishes global bank capital standards, discussed reviewing its crypto regulations in February, suggesting that reduced capital requirements could render crypto market entry more financially viable for banking institutions. Among major institutions, one bank became the first of eight U.S. banks designated as systemically important—institutions whose failure could affect the broader economy—to safely store digital currencies. If other institutions, such as Citi or State Street, announce firm go-live dates for Bitcoin custody before December 31, the perspective that banks are following a broader adoption trend will gain hard evidence.

## What the Evidence Shows

Deutsche Bank is weeks away from offering crypto custody in Germany before expanding to Asian financial centers in 2027. Custodians charge small fees and hold assets already selected by clients. Institutional adoption is expected to grow if client demand continues and global regulatory standards ease.
