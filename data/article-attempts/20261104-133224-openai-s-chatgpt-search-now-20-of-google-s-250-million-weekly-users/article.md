## Search‑Like Traffic Is Now a Fifth of Google’s
OpenAI says that ChatGPT now handles roughly one search‑like query for every five that Google handles, which translates to about 20 percent of Google’s search volume. The figure comes directly from Adam Fry, OpenAI’s ChatGPT consumer product lead, who presented the data at an OpenAI‑hosted dinner in New York.

## User Base Has Leapt to 1.2 Billion
OpenAI’s own statements put ChatGPT’s weekly active users at 1.2 billion by late September, up from 1 billion in August. The company added 200 million new users in just 60 days. These numbers illustrate the rapid uptake of the platform.

## A Growing Image‑Creation Engine
Beyond text, ChatGPT’s image tool generates about 1.5 billion images per week, showing that the service is being used for visual content as well.

## Merging Chat and Work Modes
Fry also revealed plans to combine ChatGPT’s chat and work modes, removing the toggle that has been a source of confusion. The change reflects a broader strategy to streamline the user experience.

## Uncertainties About the Numbers
The reported 20 percent figure does not specify the Google metric used, its geographic scope, or the period over which it was measured. Search Engine Journal notes that the comparison is hard to evaluate until OpenAI clarifies the Google figure. Outside estimates have been lower and older, indicating that the exact scale is still unclear.

## Practical Takeaways
For businesses, the growth suggests that many consumers are turning to AI‑powered assistants for tasks traditionally handled by search engines. However, because the precise measurement methodology is unknown, stakeholders should treat the 20 percent figure as an indicative benchmark rather than a definitive market share.

## What Readers Should Watch
OpenAI’s next updates will be crucial for understanding how the company defines “search‑like queries” and how the new merged chat/work interface performs in real‑world use. Tracking these developments will clarify the true impact of ChatGPT on the search ecosystem.
