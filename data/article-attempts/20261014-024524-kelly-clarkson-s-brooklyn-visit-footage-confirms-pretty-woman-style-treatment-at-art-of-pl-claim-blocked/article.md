## The Incident Unfolds in Video
Kelly Clarkson’s trip to Art of Play in Brooklyn, a novelty store she had praised on X, was captured on the shop’s security cameras. The footage was released by Pablo Torre, host of the investigative podcast “Pablo Torre Finds Out.” In the clip, Clarkson spends about five minutes inside the store and engages with a staff member near a kaleidoscope. The video shows her standing in front of an automaton bird and speaking with an employee who points toward something by the bird, though the video does not reveal what the employee was showing.

The clip is consistent with Clarkson’s description of the visit. She posted on X that she had driven to Brooklyn to see “really rad” items she wanted to buy, only to be “looked at as if I couldn’t afford it” and told the employee could find something comparable.

## Store’s Response and Public Commentary
After the video surfaced, Art of Play issued a statement explaining that the automaton bird is permanently attached to the wall and not for sale, and that it plays a role in the shop’s magic shows. The statement was followed by a skeptical response from Clarkson, who wrote “Cool, cool” after the interaction.

The store’s statement also included a broader comment about welcoming customers, noting that they “greet everyone who walks in the door with the invitation to ‘pick things up and play’.”
