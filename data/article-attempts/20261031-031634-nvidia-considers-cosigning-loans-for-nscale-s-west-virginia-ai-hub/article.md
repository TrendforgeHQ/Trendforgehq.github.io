## Why Nvidia’s Offer Matters
Nvidia is weighing an unprecedented move to co‑sign financing for Nscale’s AI data center project in West Virginia. The proposal would see Nvidia act as a residual value guarantor, stepping in if Nscale defaulted on construction payments, taking temporary control of the facility and helping locate a buyer or new tenant. This arrangement represents a shift from Nvidia’s usual support of cloud providers, which has largely covered leases and chip purchases, not construction.

## Project Context and Scale
Nscale’s first building, a 460‑megawatt facility, is slated to use Nvidia’s next‑generation Vera Rubin chips. The contract for that building alone is worth $44.6 billion. The companies are also discussing a second deal of roughly $45 billion for another building. Nscale has struggled to launch its projects; in August it ran about 25,000 advanced AI chips, far short of the nearly 500,000 it plans to deploy. Despite these hurdles, the project won state certification in September after months of negotiations. 

## Investor Sentiment and Financing
Investors are increasingly nervous about the sustainability of AI spending, a trend that has tightened capital for data‑center construction. Under the residual value guarantee, Nvidia would step in if Nscale defaulted, a sign of how hard the AI industry is working to keep its build‑out plans afloat as investors grow jittery about the amounts of money involved. That includes $1 billion in convertible financing committed to Nscale in September.

## High Stakes for Nscale
Nscale is seeking to go public at a valuation of up to $35 billion, but concerns about its ability to deliver on ambitious data‑center projects remain. The potential Nvidia cosign could be transformative for the cloud provider, providing the financial backing that has proven difficult to secure.

## What to Watch
The outcome of Nvidia’s cosigning talks will reveal how chipmakers are adapting to a tighter capital environment and whether such guarantees become a new norm for large‑scale AI infrastructure projects. The next steps will likely involve finalizing the terms of the residual value guarantee and assessing the impact on Nscale’s IPO timeline.
