## Outage scope
On Friday, a widespread Microsoft 365 outage brought Outlook, Microsoft Teams and the Copilot AI assistant offline for users around the world. The disruption affected core functions of Teams and Copilot, while Outlook experienced connectivity problems that prevented many users from sending or receiving mail.

## Recovery progress
By early Friday, Microsoft reported that the core functions of Teams and Copilot were back online. A later update from the company on X noted encouraging recovery progress, with service availability and email delivery improving across a growing portion of affected infrastructure. Users began to see delayed emails trickling back into inboxes as services recovered.

## Impact on institutions
The outage had concrete effects on public institutions. Singapore warned its taxpayers that email capabilities had been disrupted. Courts in the Philippines and the European Court of Human Rights said the outage could cause delays in their work.

## Prior incident
The outage followed a separate Teams incident earlier Thursday that had isolated users in North and South America. That earlier event was fully resolved before the larger outage began.

## Current status
Although some apps and features remain in a degraded state, many people, including staff at The Verge, report restored access to their emails. However, Outlook mail flow may still be delayed as Microsoft works to fully fix the remaining issues.

## What to watch
Microsoft has not yet clarified the root cause of the outage or whether it is linked to the earlier Teams disruption. Users and administrators should monitor official status updates and consider using read‑only scans of Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace to verify data integrity while the service stabilizes.

## Bottom line
The global outage demonstrated how quickly core Microsoft 365 services can be affected and how recovery can be uneven across regions. Users who rely on Outlook for business communication should be aware that while connectivity may return, email delivery performance can lag for some time. Keeping abreast of Microsoft’s status updates remains the best way to gauge when full functionality is restored.
