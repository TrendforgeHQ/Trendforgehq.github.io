## New Scheduling Feature
Meta’s WhatsApp now offers a scheduling option that lets users set a delivery date and time for a message. The feature appears in a beta build for Android and, once released, will be available to the platform’s 3 billion users. The interface adds a dialog at the bottom of the chat screen where a user can tap the send button, hold it, and then choose the desired arrival time.

## How It Works
A user can schedule a message as soon as ten minutes after drafting it and up to two weeks in advance. The app provides a dedicated section that shows all pending scheduled messages and lets users manage them before they are sent. The ability is available for both one‑to‑one chats and group conversations.

## Why It Matters
The scheduling function addresses a frequent user request for time‑zone‑aware reminders, off‑hour business messages, and the ability to send birthday wishes at the optimal time. By letting people plan communications ahead of time, WhatsApp adds a layer of convenience that other messaging platforms have offered for years.

## Alternatives for Early Access
Until the feature is rolled out broadly, third‑party apps such as SKEDit Scheduling for Android and Shortcuts for iOS can schedule WhatsApp messages. These tools also support automating other mobile services, like phone calls, SMS, emails, and social‑media posts.

## What to Watch
When the scheduled‑message feature goes live, users will be able to create, review, and edit drafts from a single screen, reducing the need for third‑party tools. Observers should track how the feature handles time‑zone changes, group‑chat permissions, and any limits on message content.

## Takeaway
Meta’s addition of scheduled messages expands WhatsApp’s functionality to match long‑standing user expectations. The new tool will let people plan communications for up to two weeks in advance, a capability that brings the app in line with other major messaging services.
