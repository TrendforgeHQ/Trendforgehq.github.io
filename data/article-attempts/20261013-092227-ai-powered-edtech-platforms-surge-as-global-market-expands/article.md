## Global Market Growth and Drivers
The AI‑in‑education market reached USD 7.05 billion in 2025 and is projected to exceed USD 136.79 billion by 2035, expanding at a CAGR of 34.52% from 2026 to 2035.  The forecast attributes the rise to schools and EdTech companies integrating AI‑driven analytics, tutoring and automated assessments.  High‑speed internet penetration and the development of customized learning paths further boost adoption.

## Technology Giants and Product Segments
Major technology firms such as Tencent, Alibaba Group and Baidu are investing heavily in AI‑powered tutoring platforms, adaptive learning systems and intelligent assessment tools.  In 2025, the learning platform and virtual facilitators segment held the largest revenue share in the market, offering automated assessment, administrative efficiency, immersive learning experiences, 24/7 support, enhanced accessibility and 24/7 tutoring.

## Policy Context: India and Vietnam
India’s National Education Policy 2020 mandates the integration of AI‑related subjects—digital literacy and coding—across all school levels to prepare students for a tech‑driven economy.  Vietnam’s online education sector is shifting from a “tech pilot” phase to a “content and experience strategy” phase.  National digital transformation policies, AI development commitments and efforts to popularize digital skills in education create tangible growth opportunities for market participants.

## Local Market Dynamics in Vietnam
Vietnamese learners show strong interest in data science, AI and Chinese language courses.  Local edtech start‑ups such as Vuihoc.vn, Edupia, Moon.vn and Topica accelerate investments in AI and adaptive learning models that personalize progress for individual students.  These domestic players possess a deep understanding of local student and parent psychology, a decisive advantage in a cost‑sensitive and trust‑driven market.

## Emerging Trends and Opportunities
The market analysis highlights growing appreciation for personalized education solutions, including real‑time learner performance analytics, adaptive assessment technologies, intelligent student engagement and feedback mechanisms.  AI‑powered platforms provide customized lessons, intelligent tutoring, performance monitoring and immediate feedback, catering to individual learner requirements.  These capabilities enhance educational outcomes through continuous optimization of the learning process.

## Uncertainties and Limitations
While the projected growth is substantial, the edtech market faces hurdles such as affordability, users’ preference for free or partially paid models and the challenge of retaining learners long‑term.  The rapid pace of AI development, particularly generative models like ChatGPT, introduces new regulatory and ethical considerations that are not yet fully resolved.  Market forecasts rely on assumptions about continued investment and internet access, which could shift with macroeconomic changes.

## What to Watch Next
Stakeholders should monitor how policy initiatives—especially India’s NEP 2020 and Vietnam’s digital transformation plans—translate into concrete curriculum changes and funding for AI integration.  The continued involvement of major technology firms and the performance of local startups in adapting to local preferences will also influence market trajectories.  Finally, the impact of generative AI on content creation, assessment integrity and teacher roles remains an evolving area that will shape future adoption.

## Conclusion
The AI‑in‑education market is expanding rapidly, driven by technological investment, policy support and consumer demand for personalized learning.  Major tech firms are leading the way, while local players in Vietnam are tailoring solutions to specific cultural and economic contexts.  However, affordability, user retention and regulatory uncertainty present significant challenges that could temper growth if not addressed.
