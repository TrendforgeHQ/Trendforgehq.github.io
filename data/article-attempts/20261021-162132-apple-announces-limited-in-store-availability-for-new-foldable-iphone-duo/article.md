## Limited Walk‑In Supply
Apple has confirmed that only a limited number of iPhone Duo units will be available for walk‑in customers on Friday. The company said it will have a small supply in stores after online orders sold out quickly, pushing some delivery dates into January. Availability will differ by country, with walk‑in and online pickup options announced for the United States, Australia, Canada, Mexico, much of the UK and Europe.

## Product Details
The iPhone Duo is Apple’s first foldable smartphone. It combines a 5.4‑inch outer display with a 7.6‑inch inner screen when unfolded. The device starts at $1,999. Apple noted that it may not have enough inventory to meet walk‑in demand because of supply constraints.

## Launch Event
Apple showcased the foldable at its event held at the Steve Jobs Theater in Cupertino, California. The presentation highlighted the new device’s design and the broader iPhone lineup.

## Market Context
Apple announced the phone in September, entering a niche segment dominated by rivals such as Samsung and Huawei that have sold premium foldables for years. Analysts expect the iPhone Duo to compete in that space.

## What Customers Should Know
Customers planning to visit Apple Stores on Friday should be aware that the walk‑in supply will be limited and that availability will vary by region. Those who order online may still face extended delivery times.

## Key Takeaway
Apple’s limited in‑store rollout of the iPhone Duo reflects production constraints on a complex new product. While the phone offers a novel foldable experience and a high price point, the tight supply means that early adopters will need to act quickly to secure a device.
