## The Decision to Re‑appoint Jensen Huang
Nvidia’s board reinstated founder Jensen Huang after he was briefly ousted. The move followed revelations about Huang’s personal affairs, prompting the board to act quickly, as reported by Amna Nawaz.

## Senior‑Government Assurances and Ongoing Investigation
The board said it received assurances from senior government officials, and the president also offered support, according to Dmitri Alperovitch’s interview. Those assurances were offered even though investigations into the extent of any national‑security risk are still unfolding.

## Possible Information Flow and Chinese Links
It is unknown whether Huang may have passed any sensitive information to the U.S. government or the Chinese Ministry of State Security. A recent New York Times story alleged that a close associate of Huang, referred to as Alice Huang, had an ongoing relationship with the Chinese security agency, the Chinese equivalent of the CIA.
