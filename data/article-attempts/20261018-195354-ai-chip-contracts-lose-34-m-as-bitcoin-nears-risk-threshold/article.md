## Forced Liquidations in AI‑Chip Futures
Crypto venues reported over $33 million in liquidations linked to AI chip stocks, with most of the losses coming from leveraged long positions. The biggest single wipeout was a $7.27 million short on Binance’s contract for the Direxion Daily Semiconductor Bear 3X Shares (SOXS). The liquidations included contracts for Micron (MU), SanDisk (SNDK) and SK Hynix (SKHY) and affected more than 4,000 traders on five chip‑linked contracts, according to CoinGlass data.

## Bitcoin and Ethereum Near Liquidation Triggers
Bitcoin (BTC) traded just above its $80,862 long‑liquidation risk level, while Ethereum hovered close to its $2,354 threshold. Both cryptocurrencies were now near the next risk zone, prompting increased scrutiny among market participants. These price levels are key markers where automated liquidations can trigger a cascade of selling.

## Context and Implications
The wipeout occurred one day before the 39th anniversary of the Black Monday crash, adding a historic backdrop to the sudden market shake. The forced selling was part of a broader wave of liquidations, illustrating how leveraged positions in specific sectors can amplify volatility across crypto markets. Traders observing Bitcoin and Ethereum near their risk thresholds may want to monitor leveraged exposure and consider risk‑management strategies.

## Takeaway
The episode shows that concentrated leveraged bets on AI chip stocks can spill over into the broader crypto market, pushing major tokens toward critical liquidation levels. While the event is a snapshot of short‑term dynamics, it underscores the importance of monitoring both sector‑specific contracts and the main cryptocurrencies that can be affected by cascading liquidations.
